# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
React (Vite), Zustand, Tailwind CSS (implied/delegated)

## Users
Shoppers looking for high-end, premium products. They value quality, aesthetics, and detailed information before making a purchase.

## Product Purpose
To provide an elegant e-commerce experience that showcases luxury goods, allowing users to discover products, view detailed information, and checkout seamlessly. Success means high engagement with product details and completed premium purchases.

## Positioning
A premium shopping destination that prioritizes deep product discovery and elegant presentation over high-volume, low-friction discounting.

## Operating Context
Users browsing on both desktop and mobile devices, likely taking time to research and view high-quality imagery before committing to a premium purchase.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Public product catalog and detailed views.
- Cart state management (Zustand).
- Xendit payment integration for checkout.
- Admin dashboard for product/category management.

## Brand Commitments
Elegant and premium vibe. The design must feel high-end, sophisticated, and trustworthy.

## Evidence on Hand
Current seed data includes electronics, clothing, and books, but the presentation must elevate these to feel premium. Placeholder images exist but must be treated carefully to maintain the luxury feel.

## Product Principles
1. **Details matter:** Prioritize rich product information and clear discovery paths.
2. **Elegant restraint:** Use a sophisticated visual language that lets the premium products stand out.
3. **Trust through craft:** High-quality UI execution builds the necessary trust for premium purchases.
